# Spatial redesign

Scope: all existing web surfaces. Landing and store hero are Persuade/Experience; commerce forms and administration are Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A digital fashion atelier whose flowing silk and photographic garments connect the SaaS product to its fashion audience.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal and champagne on the SaaS landing, warm cream in the store, translucent controls and editorial serif lettering. Working screens retain familiar tables, forms and navigation.

STORY: Create a store from the landing, explore real garments in the storefront, and operate the business using existing data and controls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing headline above and across a central champagne cloth, four floating capability cards at the sides, clear store-creation action. Store: full-width cream stage, giant serif type behind a cutout editorial subject, real collection/try-on actions and a lower orbit control.

FORM: User-pinned screenshot composition and supplied design brief. No random concept selection; user references are the visual authority. Build directly against those references.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: no fabricated fit metrics, real 360 claims from single photos, business changes, or new backend dependency. Reduced motion, keyboard controls, mobile scrolling and WebGL fallback are required. Tilt stays slight on operational surfaces.
